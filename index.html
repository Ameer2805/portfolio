Game 1 · 2D actionCompleted

Hammer Havoc

Hammer Havoc is a 2D action game made with Unity and C#. The player swings a hammer to defeat crab enemies. Each crab respawns after it is defeated, so play never stops until one touches the player.

  • Player attack. The player fights with a hammer.
  • Enemy system. Crabs can be defeated and then respawn.
  • Collision detection. Handles contact between the player, the weapon and the enemies.
  • Game over. The game ends when an enemy touches the player.
  • Sound effects. A sound plays when an enemy is defeated.
